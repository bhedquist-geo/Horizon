### [BepiColombo Spacecraft Arrives in Orbit Around Mercury](https://eos.org/research-and-developments/bepicolombo-arrives-at-mercury) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The ESA-JAXA BepiColombo mission has successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey that included nine planetary flybys. The spacecraft consists of two separate units—the Mercury Planetary Orbiter and the Mercury Magnetospheric Orbiter—which will separate in December to begin comprehensive surface mapping and magnetospheric data collection in 2027.

rss · Eos \(AGU\) · Nov 21, 18:48

**「Context」** Reaching Mercury requires shedding massive amounts of orbital energy to match the planet&\#x27;s trajectory around the Sun, making the journey harder than reaching Pluto and leaving Mercury the least explored inner solar system planet. A 2024 thruster glitch delayed the arrival from December 2025 to November 2026. BepiColombo follows only two previous missions: NASA&\#x27;s Mariner 10 flybys and the MESSENGER orbiter.

**「Relevance」** This mission provides excellent context and upcoming datasets for planetary geology and space physics courses studying planetary magnetic fields, cratered surfaces, and inner solar system evolution.
