New research published in Science Advances combines over 4,000 satellite images with sea-level rise projections to evaluate flood exposure across 20 populous African coastal cities. The source reports that sinking land and rising seas will expose more than seven million people, one million buildings, and $180bn in assets to flooding by mid-century under a moderate warming scenario. Lagos and Alexandria are identified as primary subsidence hotspots, sinking at rates of 5.2mm and 6.3mm per year, respectively.

**「Relevance」** This research provides concrete statistics and regional case studies that can be integrated into teaching and research focused on GIS applications, remote sensing, geomorphology, and African urban geography.
