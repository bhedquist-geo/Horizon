# GeoAI and geospatial data science profile

Use this profile for items about applying AI and machine learning to geospatial and Earth-observation data, and for core geospatial data science tools and practice. Typical topics include GeoAI and Earth-observation foundation models, deep learning for satellite and aerial imagery, geospatial large language models and agents, cloud-native geospatial data and pipelines (for example Zarr, STAC, Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF, and GeoParquet), and notable releases or practitioner discussions of tools such as geopandas, xarray, rasterio, leafmap, geemap, CesiumJS, QGIS, and DuckDB spatial.

Typical items include research papers and preprints, news reports on new models, datasets, or platforms, announcements of significant new tool features, tutorials, and practitioner discussions.

Do not use this profile for general AI or computer vision items with no geospatial or Earth-observation angle, routine bug-fix or dependency-bump releases, or climate, geoscience, teaching, and funding items whose main subject is not geospatial technology. Items written in a language other than English are out of scope for this briefing.
