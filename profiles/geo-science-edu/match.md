# Climate, geoscience, teaching, and funding profile

Use this profile for items about climate and atmospheric science, oceans, hydrology and water resources (including drought and groundwater), geoscience and Earth-surface processes, and remote sensing applied to those science questions. Also use it for items about teaching and curriculum in geography, geoscience, GIS, and higher education, including the use of AI in teaching, and for funding opportunities, grant announcements, and policy changes affecting geoscience, geospatial, climate, and education research.

Typical items include research papers and preprints, science news and features, data and field campaign announcements, education articles and case studies, and grant or program announcements.

Do not use this profile for items whose main subject is GeoAI methods or geospatial software tools (use the geoai profile), for general political commentary, or for items not written in English, such as translated editions of English articles.
